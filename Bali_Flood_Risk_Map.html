How to find GPS coordinates from Google Maps
💡 Why coordinates? Bali addresses are often inaccurate — the same street name can exist in multiple villages (e.g. "Jl. Raya" in every district). GPS coordinates are the only way to pinpoint an exact location.
📱 Mobile (Google Maps App)
1 Open Google Maps app → find your villa location
2 Long-press on the exact spot — a red pin drops
3 Tap the coordinates at the top of the screen (e.g. -8.6523, 115.1365) to copy
4 Paste directly into the search box above ✓
🖥️ PC (Google Maps Web)
1 Open maps.google.com → find your villa
2 Right-click on the exact spot → coordinates appear at the top of the menu
3 Click the coordinates to copy, then paste here ✓
🔗 Google Maps URL (Share Link)
1 In Google Maps, tap Share → Copy link
2 Paste the entire URL — coordinates are auto-extracted
